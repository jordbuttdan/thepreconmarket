Arista and OPUS are building side by side at Highway 410 and Hurontario, with entry prices within $5,000 of each other. The difference is in the product mix and the incentives: **OPUS offers $0 development charges**, which on a 2027 closing is worth more than most décor credits, while Arista includes air conditioning.

Both are already under construction, which removes much of the timing risk.

## Comparing the builders

| Builder | Price range | Size (sq ft) | Plans available |

| Arista Homes | $799,990 – $1,289,990 | 1,615 – 3,000 | 12 of 17 |
| OPUS Homes | $804,990 – $1,233,899 | 1,408 – 3,160 | 11 of 23 |

Same streets, same amenities, same commute. Compare the base specification and the deposit terms alongside the price, because that is where the real difference sits. [How to assess a builder](/guides/builder-due-diligence-guide).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$799,990 to $1,289,990** across 40 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $799,990 | At $1,289,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,999** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,999** | **$21,001** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- Arista: $20,000 on signing, $20,000 at 30 and 60 days, $10,000 at 90, 120 and 180 days
- OPUS townhomes: $20,000 on signing, $15,000 at 30, 90 and 150 days, $10,000 at 180 and 210 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Decor credits (both builders)
- Free appliances (both builders)
- Free assignment (both builders)
- Free air conditioning (Arista)
- $0 development charges (OPUS)

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Before you sign at SouthCal

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign.** [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Confirm your HST rebate eligibility in writing**, especially the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer)
