Arista and OPUS are building side by side at Highway 410 and Hurontario, with entry prices within $5,000 of each other. The difference is in the product mix and the incentives: **OPUS offers $0 development charges**, which on a 2027 closing is worth more than most décor credits, while Arista includes air conditioning.

Both are already under construction, which removes much of the timing risk.

## Comparing the builders

| Builder | Price range | Size (sq ft) | Plans available |

| Arista Homes | $799,990 – $1,289,990 | 1,615 – 3,000 | 12 of 17 |
| OPUS Homes | $804,990 – $1,233,899 | 1,408 – 3,160 | 11 of 23 |
| DECO Homes | $874,990 – $1,429,990 | 1,646 – 3,822 | 9 of 17 |

DECO released first, in September 2025, and carries the largest product; Arista and OPUS followed in February 2026. Same streets, same amenities, same commute. Compare the base specification and the deposit terms alongside the price, because that is where the real difference sits. [How to assess a builder](/guides/builder-due-diligence-guide).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$799,990 to $1,429,990** across 57 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $799,990 | At $1,429,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,999** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,999** | **$7,001** |

Note what the top of that band does. DECO's largest plans sit close to **$1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate hits zero, and a lot premium plus upgrades can carry you across it. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
