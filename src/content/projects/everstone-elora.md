**Cachet Homes launched Everstone on October 12, 2026, from $549,990.** It is a freehold community at Woolwich Street East and Irvine Street in Elora with four product types: 20 foot townhomes, 27 foot detached links, and 36 and 42 foot detached homes, including bungalows. There are **30 plans** from 1,264 to 3,685 sq ft.

Standard finishes include quartz kitchen counters, engineered wood on the main floor, central air, a garage EV conduit and a smart-home package.

## The price is net of HST, for principal residents

Cachet quotes prices **net of HST for principal residents only**. That means the price already counts on the rebates a qualifying owner-occupier would assign to the builder. An investor, or anyone who will not live in the home, should expect a higher price. At these prices the Ontario rebate is roughly **$44,000 to $80,000**. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits

The 36 foot detached homes ask **$85,000 over six months**: $5,000 on signing, $15,000 at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days, and $10,000 at 150 and 180 days. That is inside the Tarion cap. The towns, links and 42 foot homes each have their own structure.

## Incentives

A design studio credit of **$5,000** on towns and links or **$10,000** on detached homes, free central air, and an offer of **$0 closing costs with no development charge increases**. Read the conditions on that last one in the agreement: it caps what the builder passes on, it does not remove every closing cost. [Development charge caps](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap).

## Location

Elora is a heritage village on the Grand River in Centre Wellington, about 25 minutes north of Guelph by Highway 6. The Walk Score is 9, so plan on driving. Closings start in **spring 2028**.

Details on this page come from Cachet's October and November 2026 materials as reported by Red Bricks.
