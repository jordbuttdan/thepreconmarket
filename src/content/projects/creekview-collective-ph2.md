Creekview Collective Towns Phase 2 is Branthaven Homes's freehold townhome release at Derry Road & Fourth Line in Nelson, Milton, priced from $719,990 to $994,990 across 17 floor plans.

Worth noting the **levy cap** in the current incentives. On a closing years out an uncapped development charge clause is open-ended, so a cap is usually worth more than the décor credit sitting next to it.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$105.75 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $719,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$719,990 to $994,990** across 17 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $719,990 | At $994,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$57,599** | **$79,599** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
