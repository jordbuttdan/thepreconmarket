Angus Glen Skye Towns is Kylemore Communities's freehold townhome release at Angus Glen Boulevard & Major Mackenzie Drive East in Angus Glen, Markham, priced from $1,099,000 to $1,350,000 across 5 floor plans.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$199.99 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,099,000 to $1,350,000** across 5 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,099,000 | At $1,350,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$40,100** | **$15,000** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
