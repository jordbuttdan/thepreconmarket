**Kylemore Communities launched Angus Glen Skye Towns on October 23, 2026, from $1,099,000.** These are 18 foot, three-storey townhomes at Angus Glen Boulevard and Major Mackenzie Drive East in Markham: five three-bedroom, 2.5-bath designs from 1,665 to 2,050 sq ft. The builder notes that its stated areas include open-to-above space, so usable floor area is a little less.

Red Bricks shows **no plans available** from the October 17 price list, so ask what is actually left before you plan around a model.

## The deposit is the number to plan for

Skye asks **15%**: $10,000 with the offer, $25,000 at 30, 90 and 120 days, the balance to 10% at 210 days, and a further **5% when the building permit is issued**. On a $1,099,000 home that is about **$165,000**. Deposits are payable to the builder's lawyers in trust, but Tarion protects a freehold deposit only up to $100,000. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Pricing and the rebate

Prices are **inclusive of HST, net of the rebates the builder expects**. Every price here is between $1,000,000 and $1,500,000, where the Ontario rebate is a flat **$80,000**. A first-time buyer could add about **$40,100 federally at $1,099,000** and **$15,000 at $1,350,000**. If you do not qualify for what the price assumes, you pay the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The decor package, valued by the builder at $70,000, and the $7,500 smart-home package have **no cash value** and are not deducted from the price.

## The monthly fee

The price list sets a fee of **$199.99 a month**. Kylemore's earlier Angus Glen townhomes were sold as freehold parcels of tied land, and a fee of this size points to the same structure here. Confirm it in the agreement, because a POTL can also bring a 10-day statutory rescission right. [What a POTL is](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

## Location

Skye is at **Angus Glen Boulevard and Major Mackenzie Drive East**, north of Unionville, near the Angus Glen golf club and community centre. Highway 404 is a short drive west.

Details on this page come from Kylemore's October 17, 2026 price list as reported by Red Bricks.
