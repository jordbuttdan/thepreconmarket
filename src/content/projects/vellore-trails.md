The headline number here is not the price, it is the **Walk Score of 89**. Almost every new low-rise community in this market scores between 1 and 30, because they are built on greenfield land where the shops and services arrive years later. 89 means this is infill in an established part of Vellore Village, with the amenities already there. For a buyer who does not want to spend four years driving to everything, that is worth more than most incentive packages.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$764,593 to $1,033,990** across 16 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $764,593 | At $1,033,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$61,167** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$38,229** | **$46,601** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- Phase 2 street towns: $20,000 on signing then $20,000 at 30, 60, 90, 120 and 150 days ($120,000)
- Phase 2 urban towns: $90,000 total
- Two further structures by product

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Location

Vellore Trails is at **Weston Road & Comdel Boulevard** in Vellore Village, Vaughan. Walk Score 89, Transit Score 38.

That is a relatively walkable score for new low-rise, which usually means infill or an established area rather than a greenfield site.

## Before you sign at Vellore Trails

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).
