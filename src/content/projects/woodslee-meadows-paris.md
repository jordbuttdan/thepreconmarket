**Pinevest Homes launched Woodslee Meadows on November 12, 2026, from $505,900.** It is a townhome community facing Woodslee Avenue and Hartley Avenue in Paris, beside parkland, with three designs: The Willow and The Cedar, which are two-storey towns in standard and large formats, and The Aspen, a three-storey back-to-back. Plans run from 1,379 to 2,333 sq ft.

Main floors are 9 feet, the garage has a camera-equipped opener and a rough-in for an EV charger, and some plans offer a finished basement.

## Pricing and the rebate

At about **$343 per sq ft**, this is one of the lower entry points for a new freehold-style town within reach of the 403. Every home is under $1,000,000, so a qualifying buyer gets the full 8% Ontario rebate, roughly **$40,000 to $54,000**, and a first-time buyer may add about **$25,000 to $34,000** federally. Red Bricks does not say whether the listed prices already assume a rebate, so ask. [How the Ontario rebate works](/faq/ontario-enhanced-new-housing-rebate).

## Deposits

**10% over five months**: $5,000 on signing, 2.5% at 30, 90 and 120 days, and the balance at 150 days. That is about $50,600 at the low end and $68,000 at the top, both inside the Tarion cap. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Back-to-backs and tenure

Back-to-back towns share a rear wall and often sit on a common-elements road or walkway. Some builders sell them as a **POTL** with a monthly fee; Red Bricks lists no fee here. Confirm the tenure in the agreement. [What a POTL is](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

## Location

Paris is in the County of Brant on the Grand River, about 10 minutes to Brantford and Highway 403. The Walk Score is 46, so some errands are walkable but most buyers will drive.

Details on this page come from Pinevest's launch materials as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 2026.
