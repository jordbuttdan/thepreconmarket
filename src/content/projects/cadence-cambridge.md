**Fusion Homes released Cadence on November 16, 2026, with two-storey towns from $624,990 and detached homes from $789,990.** It is a community of about 160 homes on the former Forbes Estate lands at 155 Guelph Avenue in Hespeler Village, Cambridge. Fusion broke ground in September 2026.

| Home type | Size | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two-storey townhome | 1,375 to 1,430 sq ft | **$624,990** |
| 30' detached | 1,700 to 2,400 sq ft | **$789,990** |
| 40' detached | 2,350 to 3,300 sq ft | **$1,064,990** |

Prices are before lot premiums. Every price list shows **$0 development charges, $0 levies and $0 Tarion enrolment fees**. The site also includes stacked urban towns, which are outside what this site covers.

## The price already includes the rebate

Fusion's prices **include the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**, which the buyer assigns to the builder. It is 8% of the price up to $1,000,000, so about **$50,000 on a $624,990 town**. If you don't qualify, you pay it back at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits

**10% in total**: $5,000 on signing with the balance to 2.5%, then 2.5% at 30, 60 and 90 days. Tarion protects 10% of the price up to $100,000 on homes over $600,000, so the deposit is **fully covered up to $1,000,000**. On the top 40' home at $1,194,990, about **$19,500 sits above the cap**. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Fees

The price lists show an estimated **private community fee of $76.45 a month** for the towns and detached homes, with the first two years free on the detached homes. Tenure has not been published, so confirm in the agreement whether it is a POTL and whether a rescission right applies. [How POTL fees work](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

Occupancy has not been announced. Details on this page come from Fusion's release as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 18, 2026.
