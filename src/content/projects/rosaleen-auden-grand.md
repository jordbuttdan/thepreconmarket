Two things here are genuinely unusual. At **3,202 sq ft starting size and roughly $377 per square foot**, these are the largest townhomes and the best value per foot of anything on this site, and a **finished basement is included** rather than sold as a $50,000 upgrade.

The builder also advertises an **appraised value protection program**. Appraisal shortfall is the risk that ends pre-construction purchases, so a builder offering to stand behind value is worth reading carefully. Ask exactly what it covers and get it in writing.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,198,800 to $1,268,800** across 5 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,198,800 | At $1,268,800 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$30,120** | **$23,120** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
