Two caps here are worth more than the $30,000 design credit sitting beside them: **development charges capped at $3,000 and closing costs capped at $7,500**. On a Spring 2028 closing an uncapped levy clause is genuinely open-ended, so a cap at that level is unusually buyer-friendly.

Note the deposit, though. **$250,000 across 150 days** is among the heaviest and fastest schedules on this site, and well beyond Tarion's $100,000 freehold protection ceiling.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,789,990 to $2,154,990** across 22 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,789,990 | At $2,154,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$33,601** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$0** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The top of this range, $2,154,990, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Deposit

- $50,000 on signing
- $50,000 at 60, 90, 120 and 150 days
- $250,000 total

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- $30,000 design studio credit
- Luxury appliance package
- Development charges capped at $3,000
- Closing costs capped at $7,500 (municipal charges and Tarion excluded)
- Assignment permitted for a $10,000 fee

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

Balmoral Estates is at **Pine Valley Drive & Teston Road** in Pine Valley, Vaughan.

Most new low-rise is car-dependent at launch. The retail, transit and services that make an area convenient generally arrive years after the homes do, and sometimes differently than the plan showed. Worth walking the area yourself rather than relying on a score.

## Before you sign at Balmoral Estates

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign.** [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Confirm your HST rebate eligibility in writing**, especially the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer)
- **Ask for a cap on development charges** if one is not already offered. [Why](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap)
- **Decide your lot priorities in advance.** [Choosing a lot](/guides/choosing-a-lot-new-low-rise)
- **Register before your first visit.** [Why it costs you nothing](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon)
