At roughly **$790 per square foot** this is the most expensive product covered on this site, and the fees reflect it: a POTL of about **$301 a month** against the $100 to $150 typical elsewhere. What you get for it is South Oakville, a rooftop terrace, and an optional elevator.

Occupancy is **Spring 2029**, which is a long runway. Model your financing accordingly.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,499,990 to $1,549,990** across 3 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,499,990 | At $1,549,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$72,001** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$1** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The top of this range, $1,549,990, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

Kerr Village Towns is at **211 Lakeshore Road West** in Kerr Village, Oakville.
