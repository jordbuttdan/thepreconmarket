Kingshaven is Kingsmen Group Inc.'s freehold townhome release at Highway 48 & 19th Avenue in Rural Markham, Markham, priced from $829,990 to $1,159,990 across 21 floor plans.

Worth noting the **levy cap** in the current incentives. On a closing years out an uncapped development charge clause is open-ended, so a cap is usually worth more than the décor credit sitting next to it.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$169 per month for the first year**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $829,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$829,990 to $1,159,990** across 21 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $829,990 | At $1,159,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$66,399** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$41,499** | **$34,001** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- $10,000 on signing
- $20,000 at 30, 90 and 180 days
- $10,000 at 240 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).
