Townhomes of Little Rouge is Camcos Living's freehold townhome release at Donald Cousens Parkway & Ninth Line in Box Grove, Markham, priced from $768,990 to $1,144,990 across 14 floor plans.

At the entry end, $768,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$768,990 to $1,144,990** across 14 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $768,990 | At $1,144,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$61,519** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$38,449** | **$35,501** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $20,000 on signing
- $20,000 at 30, 90, 150 and 210 days
- $20,000 on occupancy

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Nest Collection limited offer: $43,000 closing credit on Oriole, $45,000 on Sparrow, limited time and limited sales

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

Townhomes of Little Rouge is at **Donald Cousens Parkway & Ninth Line** in Box Grove, Markham.

## Before you sign at Townhomes of Little Rouge

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).
