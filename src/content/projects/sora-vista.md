These are **pre-sited homes already under construction**, with closings quoted at roughly seven to eight months rather than the usual three or four years. That removes most of the financing risk that makes pre-construction uncomfortable, because your mortgage is underwritten against something close to today's rates rather than a date nobody can forecast.

At $1,950,990 to $2,439,990 the whole range sits above the $1,850,000 line, so only the standard $24,000 Ontario rebate applies and the federal first-time buyer rebate is zero.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,950,990 to $2,439,990** across 13 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,950,990 | At $2,439,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$0** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$0** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The top of this range, $2,439,990, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Deposit

- 42' homes: $50,000 on signing then $50,000 at 30, 60 and 90 days ($200,000)
- 45' homes: $50,000 on signing then $50,000 at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days ($250,000)

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Before you sign at Sora Vista

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign.** [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Confirm your HST rebate eligibility in writing**, especially the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer)
- **Ask for a cap on development charges** if one is not already offered. [Why](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap)
- **Decide your lot priorities in advance.** [Choosing a lot](/guides/choosing-a-lot-new-low-rise)
