**Walk-out and look-out basement lots** are offered here at a premium. Of all the premiums on a builder price list, a walkout is the one most consistently worth paying, it changes what the house is, cannot be added later, and resale buyers pay for it. [Which premiums hold value](/faq/lot-premiums-explained).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$789,900 to $1,299,900** across 17 floor plans. Here is what the current programs are worth at each end of that range, assuming you qualify:

| | At $789,900 | At $1,299,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,192** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,495** | **$20,010** |

**Read the price list carefully here.** The builder's advertised prices are stated **net of the enhanced HST rebate**, that is, they already assume you qualify and that you assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you turn out not to qualify, or the rebate is reduced or denied, **you are responsible for the difference at closing**, in certified funds. At this price point that is a five-figure exposure, and at the top of the range it approaches $63,192. This is not unusual and it is not a trick, but it is the single most important thing to confirm before you sign. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

At the entry end, $789,900 sits **below $1,000,000**, the full 8% Ontario rebate band, and the most favourable position in the whole structure.
