Creekview Collective Towns is Branthaven Homes's freehold townhome release at Derry Road & James Snow Parkway in Trafalgar, Milton, priced from $799,990 to $1,013,326 across 12 floor plans.

Worth noting the **levy cap** in the current incentives. On a closing years out an uncapped development charge clause is open-ended, so a cap is usually worth more than the décor credit sitting next to it.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$99.99 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $799,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$799,990 to $1,013,326** across 12 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $799,990 | At $1,013,326 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,999** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,999** | **$48,667** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- 20' rear lane and traditional towns: $5,000 on signing
- $10,000 at 30, 90, 120, 150, 180, 240 and 270 days
- $5,000 at 330 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
