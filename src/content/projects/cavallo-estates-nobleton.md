**Treasure Hill Homes released pricing at Cavallo Estates on November 19, 2026, from $999,900.** It is a freehold detached community at Highway 27 and Fairmont Ridge Trail in Nobleton, King Township, with a rare mix of bungalows alongside two-storey homes. There are 25 plans across five collections, from 1,845 to 5,645 sq ft, with those figures counting finished basement space.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Price | **$999,900 to $2,199,900** |
| Sizes | 1,845 to 5,645 sq ft, incl. finished basement |
| Plans | 25, two to five bedrooms |
| Deposit | $100,000 over ten months (Atessa, Manor) |

## Five collections

- **Atessa**: bungalows with two-car garages
- **Manor**: two-storey homes with two-car garages
- **Waterford**: bungalows and bungalofts with three-car garages
- **Westport**: homes with three-car garages
- **Worthington**: estate homes with three-car garages and 10 foot main floors

Prices are for the base elevation. Other elevations add $10,000 to $80,000 plus HST, depending on the collection, and lot premiums may apply. [How lot premiums work](/faq/lot-premiums-explained).

## What's included

Development charges are **capped at $10,000**. The launch package also lists smooth ceilings, a smart home package, an 8'6" poured basement, and a finished basement on applicable Atessa and Manor models. Check which items apply to the model and lot you choose. [How levy caps work](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap).

## Deposits and Tarion

On Atessa and Manor the deposit is **$20,000 on signing, $20,000 at 60 days, then $7,500 every 30 days to 300 days**, $100,000 in total. Tarion covers 10% of the price up to $100,000 on homes over $600,000, so on homes of $1,000,000 or more the full deposit is **within Tarion coverage**. The larger collections have their own structures; ask for them before you sign. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Rebate

For agreements signed by March 31, 2027, the Ontario enhanced HST rebate is a flat **$80,000** on a primary residence priced from $1,000,000 to $1,500,000. It shrinks above $1,500,000 and is gone at $1,850,000, so the top of this range sees no Ontario rebate. Ask how the builder credits it.

## Location

Nobleton is a small village in King Township, along Highway 27 north of Vaughan. The Walk Score is 44, so plan on driving for most errands.
