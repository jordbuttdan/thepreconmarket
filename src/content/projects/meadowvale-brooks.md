One detail from the builder's own material is worth repeating, because it is unusual: they state that **every home has been appraised at list price by a major Canadian bank**. On a pre-construction purchase the appraisal is normally the risk nobody can price, so a builder volunteering it is a meaningful signal. Ask to see it in writing.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,174,990 to $2,518,490** across 16 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,174,990 | At $2,518,490 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$32,501** | **$0** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

The top of this range, $2,518,490, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Deposit

- 32' detached: $15,000 on signing
- $20,000 at 30 days
- $35,000 at 60, 90, 120, 180 and 270 days
- Two further structures by lot width

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
