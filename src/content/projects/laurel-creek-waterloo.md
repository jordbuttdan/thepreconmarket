Fifty plans, and **every one of them is under $1,000,000**. That puts the entire community in the best rebate band there is: the full 8% Ontario rebate with no first-time buyer test, plus the full federal 5% for first-time buyers. At $953,973 that is roughly $76,300 provincially and $47,700 federally.

The deposit schedule is genuinely generous, $20,000 on signing then three more at 90, 150 and 240 days, which spreads $80,000 across eight months.

Two cautions. Prices are Mattamy's **"Price After Rebate"** figures, so they assume you qualify. And the park and proposed school on the Phase 1 plan are **subject to approvals**, which is the standard language and should be read as standard language: amenities on a site plan are an intention, not a commitment. [What a site plan does and does not promise](/faq/site-plan-changes-pre-construction).

Status is Launching Soon, so this is a register-now, pick-later situation.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$623,000 to $953,973** across 50 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $623,000 | At $953,973 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$49,840** | **$76,317** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$31,150** | **$47,698** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
