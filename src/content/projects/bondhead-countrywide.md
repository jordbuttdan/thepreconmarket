Read the deposit schedule before anything else. **$100,000 on signing and $50,000 at 30 days** is $150,000 inside a month, and it is the largest deposit on this site by a wide margin.

It also exceeds your warranty coverage. [Tarion's freehold deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario) caps at **$100,000**, so roughly **$50,000 of that deposit is not protected** by the warranty if the builder fails to close. CountryWide is an established builder and this is not a prediction, it is simply the arithmetic, and it is the kind of thing your lawyer should be securing in the agreement rather than you discovering later. [What a lawyer looks for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction).

On rebates at $1,349,990: the Ontario rebate is **flat at $80,000**, and a first-time buyer's federal rebate has tapered to roughly **$15,000** on the way to zero at $1,500,000.

One of four plans remains available and the community is already under construction, so closing timelines here are shorter than a typical pre-launch.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,349,990 to $1,349,990** across 4 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,349,990 | At $1,349,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$15,001** | **$15,001** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $100,000 on signing
- $50,000 at 30 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Before you sign at Bondhead

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign.** [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Confirm your HST rebate eligibility in writing**, especially the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer)
- **Ask for a cap on development charges** if one is not already offered. [Why](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap)
- **Decide your lot priorities in advance.** [Choosing a lot](/guides/choosing-a-lot-new-low-rise)
- **Register before your first visit.** [Why it costs you nothing](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon)
