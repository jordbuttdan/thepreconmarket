A **four-home boutique release in central Burlington**, which is close to the only new freehold product in a city almost entirely built out. The fee here is quoted per square foot rather than as a flat amount, so work out your own monthly figure before comparing it against a flat-fee community.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$899,000 to $1,040,000** across 4 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $899,000 | At $1,040,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$71,920** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$44,950** | **$46,000** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The Ghent Collective is at **2170 Ghent Avenue** in Brant, Burlington.
