**272 townhomes** by Starlane off Warden Avenue between Elgin Mills and Major Mackenzie. At that size this is a staged release rather than a single launch, which means several opportunities to buy but also several price increases between phases.

Pricing and floor plans are not yet published.

## What registering gets you

Builder price lists, floor plans, lot availability and deposit structures are released to registered buyers before a public opening, not after. On low-rise that ordering matters more than it sounds, because lots are allocated in the order people register and the best ones go on the first day.

Most builders also require your broker to be registered **at or before your first sales office visit** for representation to be recognised. Visit unrepresented and you usually cannot add it afterwards. [Why that matters](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon).

The Ontario rebate window is the other reason timing matters here. Up to $80,000 of provincial HST relief applies to agreements signed before **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date rather than the closing date. [How the rebates work](/hst-rebate).

## Location

Unity Towns is at **Warden Avenue & Elgin Mills Road East** in Rural Markham, Markham.

Most new low-rise is car-dependent at launch. The retail, transit and services that make an area convenient generally arrive years after the homes do, and sometimes differently than the plan showed. Worth walking the area yourself rather than relying on a score.

## Before you sign at Unity Towns

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign.** [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Confirm your HST rebate eligibility in writing**, especially the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer)
- **Ask for a cap on development charges** if one is not already offered. [Why](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap)
- **Decide your lot priorities in advance.** [Choosing a lot](/guides/choosing-a-lot-new-low-rise)
- **Register before your first visit.** [Why it costs you nothing](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon)
