**Kingsmen Group released Phase 3 of Sugarwood on October 26, 2026, from $489,990.** These are freehold 20 foot townhomes at 230 Colborne Street West in Lindsay: seven two-storey designs from 1,279 to 2,026 sq ft, each with a single-car garage and an unfinished basement. The builder states there are **no condo or POTL fees**.

Red Bricks shows **no plans available** on the current price list, so ask what is left or when the next release is before you plan around a model.

## The price assumes the rebate

Kingsmen's prices are quoted **based on the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**, which the buyer must qualify for and assign to the builder on closing. If you do not qualify, HST is added at closing. At these prices that rebate is roughly **$39,000 to $47,000**, and a first-time buyer may also qualify federally. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits

**10% over five months**: $5,000 with the agreement, $10,000 at each of months 1 to 4, and the balance at month 5. That is about $49,000 at the low end, inside the Tarion cap. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## What is included, and what costs extra

The bonus package includes five appliances, **$5,000 in decor dollars** and closing adjustments capped at **$7,500**. The standard finishes are modest: laminate counters and broadloom on the bedroom level. The hot-water tank is a **rental**, and the driveway and Tarion enrolment are closing adjustments.

## Location

Lindsay is the main town of Kawartha Lakes, about 90 minutes from Toronto by Highway 35 and the 401 or 407. The Walk Score is 35. Closings start in **September 2028**.

Details on this page come from Kingsmen's October 2026 materials as reported by Red Bricks.
