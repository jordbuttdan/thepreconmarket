Unionglen is the rarest thing in pre-construction: a genuine like-for-like comparison. **Seven builders are selling into one community**, on the same streets, with the same amenities and the same commute. Everything that differs is the thing you are actually buying.

That makes it the easiest place in the GTA to see what a builder's name is worth. Fieldgate opens at $834,504 and Forest Hill tops out at $1,449,990, for homes on the same block. Some of that is size and product type. Some of it is specification. Some of it is simply brand.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$834,504 to $1,449,990** across 138 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $834,504 | At $1,449,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$66,760** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$41,725** | **$5,001** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- Most builders: $20,000 on signing then four to five further instalments
- Greenpark: $20,000 on signing, $25,000 at 60 and 90 days, $30,000 at 120 days
- Madison: balance to 8% at 240 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Before you sign at Unionglen

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).
