Two unusual things here. The deposit is **$20,000 on signing with the balance to 10% not due for a full year**, which is about as gentle a schedule as exists in this market. And the advertised bonus package is **$240,000**, which is large enough that you should read exactly what it comprises and get every line of it written into an amendment before relying on it.

At 7,102 sq ft at the top, these are the largest homes covered on this site.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,198,900 to $2,999,900** across 9 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,198,900 | At $2,999,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$30,110** | **$0** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

The top of this range, $2,999,900, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Location

Royal Castlemore is at **Castlemore Road & McVean Drive** in Castlemore, Brampton.
