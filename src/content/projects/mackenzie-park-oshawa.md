**Brightstone released Phase 1 pricing at Mackenzie Park on November 14, 2026, from $599,990.** It is an infill community of 89 freehold townhomes, semis and detached homes on a former school site beside MacKenzie Park in Central Oshawa, which means established streets rather than a greenfield subdivision. There are 21 plans from 1,603 to 3,180 sq ft, with occupancy planned for 2029.

Phase 1 pricing came in **below the builder's earlier indications** of $799,000, $899,000 and $999,000:

| Home type | Phase 1 from |
| --- | --- |
| Traditional townhome | **$599,990** |
| 25' semi | **$699,990** |
| 35' single | **$899,990** |

Main floors have 10 foot ceilings and upper floors 9 foot. Phase 1 also comes with a 5-piece appliance package, a smart home package, $5,000 in decor credits, free assignment and development charges capped at $5,000. [How levy caps work](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap).

## The price already includes the rebate

Brightstone's listed prices **include the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**, and the buyer assigns it to the builder. The Ontario rebate is 8% of the price up to $1,000,000, so it is worth about **$48,000 on a $599,990 townhome** and about **$72,000 on an $899,990 single**. If you do not qualify, for example because the home will not be your or a relation's primary residence, expect to pay that back at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits

**10% over ten months**: $5,000 on signing, the balance to 1% at 30 days, then 1% every 30 days to 300 days. Tarion covers up to $60,000 on a home at or below $600,000 and 10% of the price up to $100,000 above that, so a 10% deposit here is **fully within Tarion coverage**. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Fees

These are freehold homes and Red Bricks lists **no POTL fee**. The hot water tank is a rental; the monthly cost has not been published, so ask for it before you sign.

## Location

Mackenzie Park is at **65 Athabasca Street** in Central Oshawa, beside the park it is named after. The Walk Score is 76, high for new freehold, so daily errands are realistic on foot.

Details on this page come from Brightstone's Phase 1 release as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 15, 2026.
