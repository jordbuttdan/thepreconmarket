Two things here are worth more than the incentives. **Walk-out basements and ravine-facing pie lots** are both on offer, and those are the premiums that hold their value because they cannot be added later. And at 32 towns plus 4 semis this is a genuinely small community, which usually means fewer years of construction traffic than a 500-home subdivision.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$999,900 to $1,369,900** across 11 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $999,900 | At $1,369,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$79,992** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$49,995** | **$13,010** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
