**Select lots back onto protected greenspace and Levi's Creek.** That is the premium worth paying for here: a permanently protected rear boundary cannot be manufactured and resale buyers pay for it, unlike most premiums on a price list.

Note the availability though. Only one of nine plans remains, so this is a late-phase opportunity rather than a fresh release.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,119,990 to $1,339,990** across 9 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,119,990 | At $1,339,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$38,001** | **$16,001** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
