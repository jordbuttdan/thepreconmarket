Arkfield's release here is traditional two and three-storey townhomes with a **finished basement included**, sodded rear yards and 9-foot ceilings on all three floors, plus development charges capped at $10,000. Only four of fourteen plans remain, so this is a late-phase opportunity rather than a fresh release.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$799,990 to $1,039,990** across 14 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $799,990 | At $1,039,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,999** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,999** | **$46,001** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

Wellington Place is at **200 Wellington Street West** in King West, Aurora.
