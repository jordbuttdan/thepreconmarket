The incentive package here is unusually substantial and unusually specific: **$0 development charges**, a features package the builder values at over $40,000 including Bosch appliances and a 200-amp electrical service, and a **free fourth bedroom with washroom** valued at $25,000.

Get every line of that into an amendment. A package this large is exactly the kind that gets quietly trimmed between the sales centre and the agreement.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$990,000 to $1,329,000** across 8 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $990,000 | At $1,329,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$79,200** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$49,500** | **$17,100** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- $20,000 on signing
- $15,000 at 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 and 240 days
- $10,000 on occupancy

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Development charges capped at $0
- Features package valued at $40,000+: Bosch appliances, smooth ceilings, engineered hardwood, 200-amp service, EV rough-in
- Free fourth bedroom with full washroom, stated value $25,000

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

Le Sentier Towns is at **Elgin Mills Road East & Yonge Street** in Westbrook, Richmond Hill. Walk Score 87, Transit Score 52.

That is a relatively walkable score for new low-rise, which usually means infill or an established area rather than a greenfield site.
