**Walnut Creek launched on November 11, 2026, from $599,990.** Acorn Developments is building 43 freehold townhomes on Leslie Street in Sharon, East Gwillimbury, alongside Sharon Creek: three back-to-back models and two rear-lane models, all three bedrooms, from 1,600 to 2,090 sq ft.

At about $374 per sq ft, with development charges waived, this is low pricing for new townhomes in York Region. Prices run up to $769,990 for the larger rear-lane homes, before any lot or grading premium.

## Deposits

The back-to-back townhomes ask **$5,000 on signing and $5,000 every 30 days after**, ten payments over nine months, for **$50,000 in total**. That is about 8% of the starting price, which is gentle next to the 10% to 20% common on new low-rise. The rear-lane townhomes have their own schedule. [How deposits usually work](/faq/typical-deposit-structure-pre-construction-ontario).

## The two things to check

**The HST clause.** Prices are quoted net of HST, which normally means the builder receives the HST rebates in your place. At these prices the Ontario enhanced rebate is worth up to 8% of the price, roughly $48,000 to $62,000. If the builder's pricing counts on it and you do not qualify, because the home will not be your or a relation's primary residence, expect to pay that back on closing. Read the HST schedule before you sign. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

**The POTL.** These are freehold homes tied to a common elements condominium corporation, with a fee of about **$185 a month**, waived for an initial period as a launch incentive. A POTL can also mean a 10-day statutory rescission right applies, which a pure freehold purchase never has. Ask your lawyer to confirm it on your agreement. [What a POTL is](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

## Timing and the rebate

The Ontario rebate applies only to agreements signed by **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date, not the closing date. First closings at Walnut Creek are tentatively set for September 2028. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).

## Location

Walnut Creek is at **18490 Leslie Street** in Sharon, East Gwillimbury, north of Newmarket and just west of Highway 404. The Walk Score is 27, so daily errands need a car. The back-to-back models have no rear yard; the rear-lane models have a garage off a private lane at the back.

Details on this page come from the builder's November 11, 2026 price list as reported by Red Bricks, and the Town of East Gwillimbury's planning file for 18490 and 18508 Leslie Street.
