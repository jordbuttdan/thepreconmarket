Two things make this unusual. At roughly **$322 per square foot** it is the lowest price per foot of any community covered here, and the **Easy Living collection is detached bungalows with double garages and finished basements included**, which is a genuinely scarce product. Single-storey living with no stairs is what a large and growing group of downsizing buyers actually wants, and almost nobody builds it.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$589,900 to $889,900** across 35 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $589,900 | At $889,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$47,192** | **$71,192** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$29,495** | **$44,495** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
