Preserve North is Mattamy Homes's freehold townhome release at Burnhamthorpe Road West & Sixth Line in Preserve, Oakville, priced from $964,593 to $1,128,990 across 18 floor plans.

At the entry end, $964,593 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$964,593 to $1,128,990** across 18 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $964,593 | At $1,128,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$77,167** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$48,229** | **$37,101** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $20,000 on signing
- $20,000 at 60, 120 and 180 days
- $80,000 total

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Location

Preserve North is at **Burnhamthorpe Road West & Sixth Line** in Preserve, Oakville. Walk Score 17, Transit Score 36.
