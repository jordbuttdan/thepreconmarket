The Bogart Hills is City Park Homes's freehold detached, townhome release at 16860 & 16920 Leslie Street in Leslie Street, Newmarket, priced from $719,900 to $897,900 across 15 floor plans.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$149 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $719,900 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$719,900 to $897,900** across 15 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $719,900 | At $897,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$57,592** | **$71,832** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$35,995** | **$44,895** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $10,000 on signing
- $15,000 at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days
- Balance to 10% at 150 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Free air conditioning
- Free appliances
- Free maintenance fees
- Reduced development charges

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

The Bogart Hills is at **16860 & 16920 Leslie Street** in Leslie Street, Newmarket. Walk Score 70, Transit Score 31.

That is a relatively walkable score for new low-rise, which usually means infill or an established area rather than a greenfield site.

## Before you sign at The Bogart Hills

Freehold purchases in Ontario carry **no cooling-off period**. Once the builder accepts your agreement you are bound. [Read this first](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).
