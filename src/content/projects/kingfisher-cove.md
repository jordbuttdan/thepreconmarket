Kingfisher Cove is Stonebridge Building Group's freehold townhome release at Stonebridge Boulevard in Stonebridge, Wasaga Beach, priced from $702,566 to $718,496 across 3 floor plans.

At the entry end, $702,566 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$702,566 to $718,496** across 3 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $702,566 | At $718,496 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$56,205** | **$57,479** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$35,128** | **$35,924** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
