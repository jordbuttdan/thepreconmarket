Weston Woods sits at the **same Weston and Teston intersection as [Bridgelands](/projects/bridgelands) and [UpTown Woodbridge](/projects/uptown-woodbridge)**. Three separate communities launching within weeks of each other on one corner of Woodbridge, which is unusual and genuinely useful: you can compare specification, deposit terms and incentives without changing a single thing about the location or the commute.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$849,990 to $1,559,990** across 24 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $849,990 | At $1,559,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$67,999** | **$70,401** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$42,499** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The top of this range, $1,559,990, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Location

Weston Woods is at **Weston Road & Teston Road** in Kleinburg, Vaughan.
