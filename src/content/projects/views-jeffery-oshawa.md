**Views by Jeffery Homes launched on November 11, 2026, from $861,938.** It is a community of freehold detached homes in north Oshawa, near Harmony Road North and Britannia Avenue East and minutes from Highway 407, with ten designs from 1,322 to 3,039 sq ft on 36 and 40 foot lots.

The range is unusually wide. It runs from the **Fieldview bungalow** on a 40 foot lot up to four-bedroom two-storeys, so it suits downsizers as well as growing families. The 36 foot homes have 9 foot main floors and basements; the 40 foot homes have 10 foot main floors. Every home is ENERGY STAR certified and comes with a smart-home package, and select lots have walk-up or walk-out basements.

## The price already includes the rebate

Jeffery's listed prices **include the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**. If you qualify, here is roughly what the programs are worth at each end:

| | At $861,938 | At $1,289,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **about $69,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **about $43,000** | **about $21,000** |

If you do not qualify for the Ontario rebate, expect to pay it back at closing. Ask whether the listed price also counts on the federal rebate. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits

**$100,000 over eight months**: $25,000 on signing, then $25,000 at 60 and 120 days, $15,000 at 180 days and $10,000 at 240 days. Tarion protects up to 10% of the price, capped at $100,000, so at the $861,938 starting price about $86,000 is covered and the rest relies on the builder. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Location

The sales centre and model homes are at **2208 Verne Bowen Street**. The site plan includes a park with a splash pad and a future public school. The Walk Score is 3, so expect to drive for errands; Highway 407 is a few minutes away.

Details on this page come from Jeffery Homes' launch materials as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 2026.
