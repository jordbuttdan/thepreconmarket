Every plan in this release is **four bedrooms** on 2,294 sq ft or more, so this is a family-only product with no entry option. Seaton is a new greenfield community, which means the parks, retail and recreation complex nearby are planned rather than built. Worth walking the area to see what exists today.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,017,690 to $1,292,027** across 8 floor plans. Here is what the current programs are worth at each end of that range, assuming you qualify:

| | At $1,017,690 | At $1,292,027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$48,231** | **$20,797** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, the price assumes you qualify, and a buyer who does not is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Location

Bloom Park is at **Burkholder Drive & Alexander Knox Road** in Seaton, Pickering.
