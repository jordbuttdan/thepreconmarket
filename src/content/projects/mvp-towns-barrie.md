At $560,973 this is close to the cheapest new freehold home tracked on this site, and the deposit is spread across **six payments over five months** rather than front-loaded, which is the friendliest schedule here for a buyer who is saving as they go.

One precise detail worth knowing. The total deposit is **$60,000**, and [Tarion's freehold protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario) covers 10% of the purchase price up to **$60,000** where the price is at or below $600,000, and up to $100,000 above it. So on the $560,973 plan your deposit is covered to the dollar, with nothing to spare. Add a lot premium or an upgrade that pushes the price past $600,000 and the cap rises to $100,000, so you are fine either way, but the margin is exactly zero at the base price.

Both plans sit well **below $1,000,000**, so the full 8% Ontario rebate applies with no first-time buyer test, roughly $44,900 to $48,100.
