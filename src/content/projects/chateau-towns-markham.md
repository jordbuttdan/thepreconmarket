Chateau Towns is Regency Development's freehold townhome release at 7810 McCowan Road in Milliken Mills, Markham, priced from $788,990 to $1,258,990 across 22 floor plans.

Worth noting the **levy cap** in the current incentives. On a closing years out an uncapped development charge clause is open-ended, so a cap is usually worth more than the décor credit sitting next to it.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$89.25 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $788,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$788,990 to $1,258,990** across 22 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $788,990 | At $1,258,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,119** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,449** | **$24,101** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- Village Towns: $100,000 total
- Rear Lane and Traditional Towns: $120,000 total

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Free appliances
- Free assignment
- Right to lease during occupancy
- Development charges and municipal levies capped at $9,000 plus HST

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

Chateau Towns is at **7810 McCowan Road** in Milliken Mills, Markham. Walk Score 45, Transit Score 47.

That is a relatively walkable score for new low-rise, which usually means infill or an established area rather than a greenfield site.
