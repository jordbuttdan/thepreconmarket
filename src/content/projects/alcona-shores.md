The square footage is the story here. At $639,990 for 1,858 sq ft, this is roughly **$317 per square foot**, among the lowest on this site, and the largest plan runs to 2,834 sq ft. That is detached-house space at townhome pricing.

The trade-off is Alcona. It is a lakeside Innisfil community well outside the GTA commuter band, and the deposit schedule is fast, $50,000 inside 30 days, because these are finished homes rather than a pre-launch.

Prices are quoted net of the enhanced rebate. Both the federal and Ontario rebates require the home to be your **primary residence**, and Innisfil draws recreational buyers, so this one matters more here than most places. [Primary residence rules](/faq/hst-rebate-primary-residence-requirement).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$639,990 to $779,990** across 14 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $639,990 | At $779,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$51,199** | **$62,399** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$31,999** | **$38,999** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
