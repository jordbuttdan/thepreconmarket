The deposit here is structured as **percentages rather than fixed dollar amounts**: 2% on signing then 1% a month for eight months. That is unusual, and it is worth modelling in dollars against your specific home before you sign, because a percentage schedule scales with every lot premium and upgrade you add.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$699,991 to $848,664** across 13 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $699,991 | At $848,664 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$55,999** | **$67,893** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$34,999** | **$42,433** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- 2% on signing
- 1% at 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210 and 240 days
- 10% total, scaled to purchase price

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
