Listed prices run **$1,091,900 to $1,666,900** across 14 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,091,900 | At $1,666,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$53,296** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$40,810** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

The top of this range, $1,666,900, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).
