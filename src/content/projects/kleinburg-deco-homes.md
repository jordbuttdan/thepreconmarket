DECO Homes is bringing **freehold townhomes and detached homes to Highway 27 and Kirby Road** in Kleinburg, on land beside Copper Creek Golf Club. The builder has published the location and the home types and nothing else yet: no prices, sizes, floor plans or dates.

## What the planning records show

The City of Vaughan has a subdivision on file for the south-east corner of Highway 27 and Kirby Road, at 11363 Highway 27 (file 19T-22V006, in the name of Copper Kirby Developments Limited). Its first phase is about 17 hectares: **227 detached homes, about 70 street townhouses and a park**. It is the first of four phases on the wider lands, and the later phases add several hundred more detached homes, some townhouses, an elementary school and a public park.

The address and the home types match DECO's community, but no city document reviewed names the builder, so the connection is a reasonable reading rather than a confirmed fact.

Two things in the staff reports are worth knowing before you buy here. Highway 27 may be widened to four lanes between Major Mackenzie Drive and Kirby Road, and engineering staff raised a sightline concern at the new intersection on Kirby Road. Neither is unusual for a subdivision of this size, but both affect which lots you would want. [What a site plan does and does not promise](/faq/site-plan-changes-pre-construction).

## Timing and the rebate

The Ontario rebate of up to $80,000 applies only to agreements signed by **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date. With no launch date announced, it is not yet clear whether this community will release inside that window. If it matters to your numbers, that is the first question to ask when the price list comes out. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).

## Location

Highway 27 and Kirby Road is on the north-west edge of Kleinburg. Kleinburg village, the McMichael Canadian Art Collection and the Nashville Conservation Reserve are close by, and Highways 427 and 400 are the main routes out.
