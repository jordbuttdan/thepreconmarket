The deposit structure is the unusual thing here, and it is unusually buyer-friendly: **$5,000 on signing and the balance of 5% on occupancy**. Most freehold builders want 10% inside six months. Holding $30,000 or more in your own account until occupancy instead of the builder's trust account is worth real money over a build.

It also means your exposure under [Tarion's deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario) is minimal for most of the build, since there is very little on deposit to protect.

Every plan sits comfortably **below $1,000,000**, which is the most favourable rebate band in the structure: full 8% Ontario, no first-time buyer test, and the full federal 5% on top if you qualify.

The other Terra View release in Guelph is [Hart Village Phase 2](/projects/hart-village-phase-2).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$684,867 to $817,610** across 5 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $684,867 | At $817,610 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$54,789** | **$65,408** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$34,243** | **$40,880** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
