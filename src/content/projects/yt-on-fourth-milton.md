**York Trafalgar Homes released pricing at YT on Fourth on November 25, 2026, from $822,124.** It is a limited collection of freehold townhomes and detached homes at Fourth Line and Louis St. Laurent Avenue in Milton. There are 30 plans from 1,717 to 3,794 sq ft, and the builder is planning move-ins from spring 2027, which is unusually soon for a new release.

| Sizes | 1,717 to 3,794 sq ft |
| Plans | 30, three to five bedrooms |
| Lots | 38, 55 and 60 feet |
| Deposit | $140,000 over 120 days (detached) |

## Townhomes and detached homes

- **Townhomes**: The Reid (1,717 sq ft, $822,124), The Sapp (1,908 sq ft, $875,221) and The Ray (2,665 sq ft, four bedrooms, $1,269,000)
- **38 foot detached**: seven models from 2,142 to 2,746 sq ft, $1,199,000 to $1,368,000
- **Larger detached**: The Morrisseau and The Bellefleur, 3,467 to 3,794 sq ft, $1,899,000 to $2,013,000

Detached models come in three elevations, and lot premiums may apply. [How lot premiums work](/faq/lot-premiums-explained).

## The price already includes the rebate

York Trafalgar's listed prices **include the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**. The price list shows the townhomes at $929,000 and the 38 foot detached homes at $1,329,000 before it is applied. If the home will not be your or a relation's primary residence, expect to pay the rebate back at closing. [What a net-of-rebate price means](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposits and Tarion

On the detached homes the deposit is **$35,000 on signing and $35,000 at 60, 90 and 120 days**, $140,000 in total. Tarion covers 10% of the price up to $100,000 on homes over $600,000, and every detached home here is over $1,000,000, so **about $40,000 of that deposit is above Tarion coverage**. The townhomes have a separate structure. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Location

The site is at Fourth Line and Louis St. Laurent Avenue in east Milton, with the sales centre at 1039 Fourth Line. The Walk Score is 31 and the Transit Score 33, so most errands mean driving.

Details on this page come from York Trafalgar's November 25, 2026 release as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 27, 2026.
