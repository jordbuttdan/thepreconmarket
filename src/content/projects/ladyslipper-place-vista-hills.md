Two plans, both over 3,100 sq ft, at roughly **$359 per square foot**. For a detached home on a serviced Waterloo lot that is good value on a per-foot basis, and it is the only product on this site in that size class under $1,200,000.

The deposit needs attention. **Ten per cent plus $5,000 inside 60 days** works out to roughly $117,000 on the lower plan, and [Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at $100,000](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario). So around $17,000 of your deposit sits outside the warranty's coverage. That is not a reason to walk away, it is a reason to have your lawyer look at what else secures it.

On rebates: both plans are above $1,000,000, so the Ontario rebate is **flat at $80,000** rather than 8%, and a first-time buyer's federal rebate is partially tapered, roughly $33,900 at $1,160,900. [How the bands work](/faq/ontario-enhanced-new-housing-rebate).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,121,900 to $1,160,900** across 2 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,121,900 | At $1,160,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$37,810** | **$33,910** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
