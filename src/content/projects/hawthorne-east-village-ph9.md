One pricing detail worth reading carefully: the builder's package states that a **$10,000 purchase-price discount is already included** in the published after-rebate prices and must not be deducted again. It is easy to double-count that when comparing against another builder's list, so work from the final figure rather than the advertised savings.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$658,398 to $868,133** across 9 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $658,398 | At $868,133 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$52,671** | **$69,450** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$32,919** | **$43,406** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit
