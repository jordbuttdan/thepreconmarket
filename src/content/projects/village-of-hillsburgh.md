**Ballantry Homes launched The Village of Hillsburgh on October 23, 2026, from $568,990.** It is a freehold community at 63 Trafalgar Road in Hillsburgh, in the Town of Erin, with an unusually broad mix: detached homes on 32, 38 and 45 foot lots, bungalows, traditional towns and back-to-back towns. There are **75 plans** from 1,292 to 3,991 sq ft.

Standard finishes include granite kitchen counters, an ERV, a smart-home package and central air across every collection.

## The price already includes the rebate

Ballantry's listed prices are **estimated net prices after limited-time savings and the Ontario enhanced HST rebate**. If you qualify, the price you see is close to the price you pay. If you do not, for example because the home will not be your or a relation's primary residence, expect to pay the rebate back at closing: up to **$80,000** at the top of the range. The builder's materials mention up to $130,000 in combined rebates; the federal portion is for first-time buyers only. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposits above the Tarion cap

The 38 foot detached homes ask **$110,000 over nine months**: $16,750 on signing and at 30, 60, 120 and 210 days, then $26,250 at 270 days. Tarion protects a freehold deposit only up to **$100,000**, so the last $10,000 relies on the builder alone. The towns and other lot widths have their own structures. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Back-to-back towns

Back-to-backs are the home type most often sold as a **POTL** with a monthly fee. Red Bricks lists the community as freehold, but confirm the tenure of your specific home in the agreement. [What a POTL is](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

## Location

Hillsburgh is a small village in Wellington County, north of Georgetown on Trafalgar Road and about 20 minutes from Orangeville. There is no GO service in Erin, so this is a car-first location. Construction is set to start in fall 2027, with closings from **September 2028**.

Details on this page come from Ballantry's September to November 2026 materials as reported by Red Bricks.
