Derry Lane Towns is Ballymore Homes's freehold detached, semi, townhome release at 376 & 390 Derry Road West in Derry Road West, Mississauga, priced from $769,990 to $1,289,990 across 16 floor plans.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$150 per month (townhomes)**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $769,990 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$769,990 to $1,289,990** across 16 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $769,990 | At $1,289,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$61,599** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$38,499** | **$21,001** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- Townhomes: $20,000 on signing then four further $20,000 instalments ($100,000)
- Semis and detached: five $25,000 instalments ($125,000)

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Platinum access release

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).
