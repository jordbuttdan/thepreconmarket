Being straight with you: this community is **complete and one home is left**. That is not a knock on it, a finished freehold town you can walk through and close on quickly is a genuinely different proposition from buying off a floor plan, and at $699,990 for 1,985 sq ft it prices well.

It does mean there is no lot selection, no décor appointment and no upside from an early-phase price. What you see is what you buy.

The deposit is **$50,000 on signing** in a single payment, which is steep relative to the price but comfortably inside [Tarion's protection cap](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

The **$0 development charges** matters less than usual on a completed home, since the levy risk a cap protects against has already crystallised.
