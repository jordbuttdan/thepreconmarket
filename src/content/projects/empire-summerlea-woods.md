These are **move-in-soon homes with April 2027 closings**, not a three-year build, which removes most of the financing uncertainty that makes pre-construction uncomfortable. Only three remain and most of the community is marked sold.

Note **$0 closing costs** in the incentives, which is unusual and worth more than it sounds given that closing costs on a new build commonly run $30,000 to $45,000.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$875,212 to $914,150** across 3 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $875,212 | At $914,150 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$70,016** | **$73,132** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$43,760** | **$45,707** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
