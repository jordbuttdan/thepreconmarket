One detail to read carefully. Some plans here are **live-work properties containing two residences plus a commercial space**, and the listed areas are the builder's whole-property totals rather than the size of a single home. A 2,153 sq ft figure is not 2,153 sq ft of house.

Worth noting too that Wasaga Beach is partly a recreational market. The HST rebates require the home to be a primary residence, so a second home does not qualify. [Primary residence rules](/faq/hst-rebate-primary-residence-requirement).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$795,000 to $1,073,900** across 6 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $795,000 | At $1,073,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,600** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,750** | **$42,610** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).
