Paradise Developments has announced **Upper Oakville, freehold townhomes and detached homes at Bronte Road and Dundas Street West**. The builder says details on homes and pricing are still to come.

Oakville is one of the higher-priced markets on this site, so a good share of detached product here is likely to sit above $1,000,000, where the Ontario rebate stops growing and stays flat at $80,000. [How the bands work](/faq/ontario-enhanced-new-housing-rebate).

## Timing and the rebate

The Ontario rebate of up to $80,000 applies only to agreements signed by **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date, not the closing date. With no launch date announced, it is not yet clear whether this community will release inside that window. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).

## Location

Upper Oakville is at **Bronte Road & Dundas Street West** in Oakville.
