At roughly **$364 per square foot** this is the lowest price per foot of any community covered here, and the **$60,000 total deposit** is among the lightest. Entry pricing at $596,372 sits comfortably below $1,000,000, which means the full 8% Ontario rebate and, for a first-time buyer, the full federal rebate on top.

That combination makes this one of the most accessible routes into new freehold ownership in the GTA.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$596,372 to $702,566** across 16 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $596,372 | At $702,566 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$47,709** | **$56,205** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$29,818** | **$35,128** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

## Deposit

- $10,000 on signing
- $10,000 at 30, 90, 120, 180 and 240 days
- $60,000 total

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
