The **EV charger rough-in** is a genuinely useful inclusion. It is exactly the kind of item that is cheap at construction and expensive to retrofit, the same logic that makes basement bathroom rough-ins worth buying. [Which upgrades are worth it](/faq/builder-upgrades-worth-it).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$899,990 to $979,990** across 3 floor plans. Here is what the current programs are worth at each end of that range, assuming you qualify:

| | At $899,990 | At $979,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$71,999** | **$78,399** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$44,999** | **$48,999** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, the price assumes you qualify, and a buyer who does not is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

At the entry end, $899,990 sits **below $1,000,000**, the full 8% Ontario rebate band, and the most favourable position in the whole structure.

Everpine Towns is at **25, 45 & 61 Harris Avenue** in Jefferson, Richmond Hill.
