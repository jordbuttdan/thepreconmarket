Royal Pine Homes is bringing **freehold three-storey townhomes to Castlemore Road and The Gore Road** in Brampton, from 1,600 to 2,160 sq ft, with a launch announced for November 2026.

The builder's early materials quote prices that assume the buyer receives the HST rebate. Until an official price list is out, treat any figure you see as conditional, and check whether you qualify before relying on it. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Timing and the rebate

The Ontario rebate of up to $80,000 applies only to agreements signed by **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date, not the closing date. A November 2026 launch falls inside that window. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).

## Location

Arbor Grove is at **Castlemore Road & The Gore Road** in Brampton.

Details on this page come from Red Bricks and the builder's launch materials, as of November 2026.
