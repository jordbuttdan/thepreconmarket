Red Oaks on Bayview is Trinity Point Developments's freehold detached, townhome release at Bayview Avenue & Elgin Mills Road East in Devonsleigh, Richmond Hill, priced from $989,900 to $1,099,900 across 3 floor plans.

At the entry end, $989,900 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$989,900 to $1,099,900** across 3 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $989,900 | At $1,099,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$79,192** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$49,495** | **$40,010** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $30,000 on signing
- $30,000 at 60 days
- $25,000 at 120 and 150 days
- $110,000 total

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
