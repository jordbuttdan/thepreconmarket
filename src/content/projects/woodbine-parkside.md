Woodbine Parkside is 95 Developments's freehold detached, townhome release at 1954 Major MacKenzie Drive in Cathedraltown, Markham, priced from $989,900 to $1,189,900 across 17 floor plans.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

There is a POTL or common-element fee of **$99 per month**. Where an incentive waives it, that is a waiver rather than a removal, so budget it from the year it starts.

At the entry end, $989,900 sits below $1,000,000, which is the full 8% Ontario rebate band and the most favourable position in the structure.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$989,900 to $1,189,900** across 17 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $989,900 | At $1,189,900 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$79,192** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$49,495** | **$31,010** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

## Deposit

- $20,000 on signing
- $20,000 at 30, 60 and 90 days
- Balance to 10% at 120 days

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## Current incentives

- Free appliances
- Free assignment
- $0 occupancy fee

Incentives change without notice, and a promise not written into an amendment does not exist. [How amendments work](/faq/builder-amendments-pre-construction).

## Location

Woodbine Parkside is at **1954 Major MacKenzie Drive** in Cathedraltown, Markham. Walk Score 53, Transit Score 43.

That is a relatively walkable score for new low-rise, which usually means infill or an established area rather than a greenfield site.
