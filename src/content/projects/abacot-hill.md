Abacot Hill Homes is Hallett Homes's freehold detached release at 1720 Sherwood Forrest Circle in Sheridan Park, Mississauga, priced from $1,499,990 to $2,599,990 across 10 floor plans.

Worth noting the **levy cap** in the current incentives. On a closing years out an uncapped development charge clause is open-ended, so a cap is usually worth more than the décor credit sitting next to it.

**Free assignment** is included, which is the exit most buyers never think to negotiate and cannot get later.

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$1,499,990 to $2,599,990** across 10 floor plans. Assuming you qualify, here is what the current programs are worth at each end:

| | At $1,499,990 | At $2,599,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$80,000** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$1** | **$0** |

**Read the price list carefully.** The advertised prices are stated net of the enhanced HST rebate, meaning they assume you qualify and assign the rebate to the vendor at closing. If you do not qualify, or the rebate is reduced, you are responsible for the difference at closing in certified funds. [How net-of-rebate pricing works](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning).

The top of this range, $2,599,990, is at or above $1,500,000, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Deposit

- 33' Sherwood Collection: $30,000 on signing
- $30,000 at 30 days
- $20,000 at 60, 90, 120, 180, 210 and 240 days
- Balance to 10% at 270 days
- Three further structures by collection

Each date is a contractual obligation, not a guideline. Note too that Tarion's freehold deposit protection caps at 10% of the price up to $100,000, so a larger deposit leaves an uninsured balance. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).
