Paradise Developments has announced **West Grove, freehold townhomes and detached homes at Mayfield Road and Chinguacousy Road** in northwest Brampton. It is still in the planning phase, with no prices, sizes or dates released.

Paradise is also planning [Caledon Evergreen](/projects/caledon-evergreen) at the same intersection, on the Caledon side of Mayfield Road.

## Timing and the rebate

The Ontario rebate of up to $80,000 applies only to agreements signed by **March 31, 2027**, and it runs on the signing date, not the closing date. With no launch date announced, it is not yet clear whether this community will release inside that window. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).

## Location

West Grove is at **Mayfield Road & Chinguacousy Road** in Brampton.
