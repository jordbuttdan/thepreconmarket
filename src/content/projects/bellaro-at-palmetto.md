The deposit here is the gentlest of any community covered on this site: **$10,000 on signing, then $5,000 a month for eight months**, totalling $50,000. For a buyer building the deposit out of income rather than savings, that structure is worth more than most incentive packages.

| | At $599,900 | At $739,900 |

| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$47,992** | **$59,192** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, a buyer who does not qualify is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).
