The price range here, $799,106 to $2,126,990, crosses **both** rebate cliffs. A 24-foot town receives the full 8% Ontario rebate; a 50-foot single above $1.85M receives only the standard $24,000 and nothing federally. Which collection you choose changes your rebate by more than $100,000. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## Pricing and your rebate position

Listed prices run **$799,106 to $2,126,990** across 36 floor plans. Here is what the current programs are worth at each end of that range, assuming you qualify:

| | At $799,106 | At $2,126,990 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,928** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,955** | **$0** |

Confirm with the builder whether the advertised prices are quoted net of the HST rebate. Where they are, the price assumes you qualify, and a buyer who does not is billed the difference at closing. [How this works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

Note also that the top of this range, $2,126,990, is **at or above $1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate falls to zero. Lot premiums and upgrades count toward your price, so a base price below the line can cross it at the décor appointment. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

At the entry end, $799,106 sits **below $1,000,000**, the full 8% Ontario rebate band, and the most favourable position in the whole structure.
