**City Park Homes released pricing at Holloway Towns on November 17, 2026, from $709,900.** It is a townhome community at Highway 48 and Main Street in Stouffville, backing onto conservation lands, with traditional, two-storey and back-to-back towns. All 19 plans are three-bedroom, from 1,497 to 2,153 sq ft.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Price | **$709,900 to $889,900** |
| Sizes | 1,497 to 2,153 sq ft |
| Plans | 19, all three-bedroom |
| Deposit | 10% over 150 days |

## Deposits

**$10,000 on signing, then $15,000 at 30, 60, 90 and 120 days, and the balance to 10% at 150 days.** That is about $71,000 at the starting price and $89,000 at the top. Tarion protects 10% of the price up to $100,000 on homes over $600,000, so the full deposit is **within Tarion coverage**. [What protects a deposit](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario).

## POTL fee

Holloway is listed as **POTL**. You own the home and lot, and a common elements corporation maintains shared parts, so there will be a monthly fee. It has not been published yet. A POTL purchase may also carry a 10-day rescission right. [How POTL fees work](/faq/potl-common-elements-condo-fees).

## Rebate

For agreements signed by March 31, 2027, the Ontario enhanced HST rebate is 8% of the price, about **$56,800 at $709,900**. Ask whether the listed prices already include it; if they do and you don't qualify, you pay it back at closing.

## Approvals

The Town of Whitchurch-Stouffville lists planning applications for about 176 townhomes and a commercial block on this site, and notes the proposal is **not yet approved**. Occupancy has not been announced.

Details on this page come from City Park's release as reported by Red Bricks, as of November 18, 2026.
