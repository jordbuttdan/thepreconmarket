New construction closing costs are materially higher than resale, because a builder passes through a list of charges a private seller never bills you for. Here is the complete picture on a **$1,100,000 freehold home**.

## Land transfer tax

Ontario's marginal rates:

| Portion of price | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $55,000 | 0.5% |
| $55,000 – $250,000 | 1.0% |
| $250,000 – $400,000 | 1.5% |
| $400,000 – $2,000,000 | 2.0% |
| Above $2,000,000 | 2.5% |

On $1,100,000 that is **$18,475**.

Inside the **City of Toronto** a municipal land transfer tax applies again on broadly similar bands — roughly doubling it to about **$37,000**. Most new low-rise is in the 905 (Vaughan, Markham, Brampton, Caledon, Oakville, Milton, Pickering, Whitby), so one tax rather than two. That is a real and under-discussed advantage.

**First-time buyers:** up to **$4,000** provincially and up to **$4,475** in Toronto, for up to $8,475 combined. Claimed by your lawyer at closing. Note the test is not identical to the [federal GST rebate test](/faq/first-time-home-buyers-gst-rebate) — ask about each separately. [More](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).

## The builder's adjustments

| Item | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| Development charges / levies | $5,000 – $20,000+ |
| Utility and service connections | $1,500 – $3,500 |
| Meter installations | $500 – $1,500 |
| Tarion enrolment | $600 – $1,500 |
| Grading / sodding / driveway deposits | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Utility security deposits | $300 – $1,000 |
| Builder admin, document and legal fees | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Prepaid property tax adjustment | varies |

**Development charges are the one to control.** Standard clauses pass municipal increases through to you with no ceiling, and on a closing three or four years out that is open-ended. [Negotiate a cap](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap) — the most valuable amendment available.

## Your own costs

| Item | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| Legal fees and disbursements | $2,500 – $4,000 |
| Title insurance | $400 – $900 |
| Home insurance (first payment) | $100 – $250 |
| Moving | $1,000 – $3,000 |

## The totals

**Outside Toronto:** roughly **$30,000 to $45,000**.
**Inside Toronto:** add about $18,000.

## The two that can wreck the budget

**A failed HST rebate.** If your price was [net of HST rebate](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning) and you do not qualify — [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer), [renting it out](/faq/hst-rebate-rental-investment-property), not the first occupant — the builder bills the rebate amount at closing. **Up to $80,000, in certified funds, on the day.** This dwarfs everything above.

**An appraisal shortfall.** Not a closing cost, but due the same day. If the home appraises below your purchase price the gap is cash, and there is no exit. [How this happens](/faq/mortgage-approval-closing-years-later).

## What nobody budgets

The [exclusions from the base price](/faq/what-is-included-in-a-new-low-rise-home): appliances, window coverings, fencing, rear landscaping, a deck. Commonly **$25,000 to $60,000** in the first year.

## The practical step

Ask your lawyer for an **estimate of the statement of adjustments several months out**. Most will produce one. The real statement often arrives a week or two before closing — not enough time to raise money. [More](/faq/adjustments-on-closing-new-build).
