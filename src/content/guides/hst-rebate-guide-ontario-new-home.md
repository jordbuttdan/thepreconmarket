Ontario currently offers the largest new-home tax relief in its history, and a great many buyers are working from last year's understanding of it. On a typical GTA low-rise purchase, the gap between knowing this and not knowing it is five or six figures.

HST in Ontario is 13%: a **5% federal** portion and an **8% provincial** portion. The programs below target different halves of it.

## Program 1, Federal First-Time Home Buyers' GST Rebate

Became law March 12, 2026 (Bill C-4).

- **Rebates:** the full 5% federal GST
- **Maximum:** $50,000
- **Full rebate:** up to $1,000,000
- **Phase-out:** straight line from $1,000,000 to zero at $1,500,000
- **Window:** agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before January 1, 2031
- **First-time buyer:** required

The eligibility test is the part to read carefully. Neither you **nor your spouse or common-law partner** can have **lived in a home either of you owned**, anywhere in the world, in the current calendar year or the four preceding years.

It is not "have you owned property." It is "have you lived in a home you or your partner owned." Someone who has never owned anything but lives in their partner's condo is **disqualified**. [The spousal trap](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer).

## Program 2, Ontario Enhanced New Housing Rebate

The big one, and the temporary one.

- **Rebates:** the 8% provincial portion
- **Maximum:** $80,000
- **Window:** agreements signed **April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027**
- **First-time buyer:** **not required**

| Price | ENHR |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $1,000,000 | Full 8% |
| $1,000,000 – $1,500,000 | Flat **$80,000** |
| $1,500,000 – $1,850,000 | Partial, declining |
| $1,850,000+ | None, [standard $24,000 only](/faq/hst-rebate-24000-standard) |

Two things people get wrong here.

**It is not first-time-buyer relief.** Early commentary described it that way and some explainers still do. The regulations tie it to the conditions of the existing Ontario new housing rebate, which has never had such a test. If you have owned before, you are probably still eligible. [This one matters](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

**The flat band is generous.** A $1,000,000 home and a $1,499,000 home both get $80,000.

What it *does* require: an individual buyer, the home as **primary place of residence** for you or a relation, and you as the **first occupant**. [Primary residence](/faq/hst-rebate-primary-residence-requirement).

## Program 3. The Ontario 5% top-up

A provincial payment equivalent to the 5% federal portion, up to $50,000, for buyers who **receive the ENHR**, reduced by any federal GST rebate claimed. [More](/faq/ontario-new-home-affordability-payment).

Its effect is to equalise the outcome for buyers who are not first-time buyers.

## How they combine

An $800,000 freehold townhome, inside both windows, owner-occupied:

| | First-time buyer | Has owned before |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal GST rebate | $40,000 | $0 |
| Ontario ENHR | $64,000 | $64,000 |
| Ontario top-up | $0 (offset) | $40,000 |
| **Total** | **$104,000** | **$104,000** |

Deliberately similar. The top-up fills the federal gap.

The combined maximum lands near **$130,000** around a $1,000,000 price. Above that the federal rebate phases out while the ENHR sits flat, so the total falls. [Worked examples](/faq/how-much-hst-rebate-new-home-ontario).

## The three traps

**1. The builder has your rebate.** Nearly every Ontario builder agreement prices the home HST-inclusive and requires you to **assign** the rebate at closing. You already received it as a lower price. But if you turn out not to qualify, the builder bills you the full amount **on closing day**, up to $80,000 in certified funds. [Read this](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment).

**2. Renting it out voids it.** Both enhanced rebates require owner occupancy. Change your mind and you pay the HST at closing and claim the [NRRP rebate](/faq/new-residential-rental-property-rebate) yourself, months later. [More](/faq/hst-rebate-rental-investment-property).

**3. The price cliffs.** $1,500,000 federally and $1,850,000 provincially. [Lot premiums and upgrades](/faq/lot-premiums-explained) can push you across one without you noticing. [The cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

## The deadline that is actually urgent

The ENHR window closes **March 31, 2027**, on the **signing date**, not the closing date.

On a $1.2M low-rise home, signing inside the window versus outside it is a difference of roughly **$56,000** in provincial relief alone. In low-rise that matters more than it sounds, because you cannot sign for a lot that has not been released yet, and desirable phases release on the builder's schedule, not yours.
