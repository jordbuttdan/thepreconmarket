First-time buyers are the most advantaged group in the current Ontario market and the most exposed to its traps. Both halves of that are worth taking seriously.

## What you can claim

**Federal First-Time Home Buyers' GST Rebate** — up to **$50,000**. Full 5% GST on homes to $1,000,000, phasing out to zero at $1,500,000. [More](/faq/first-time-home-buyers-gst-rebate).

**Ontario Enhanced New Housing Rebate** — up to **$80,000**. Not restricted to first-time buyers, but you get it too. Window: agreements signed April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027. [More](/faq/ontario-enhanced-new-housing-rebate).

**Ontario 5% top-up** — up to $50,000, but reduced by your federal rebate, so as a first-time buyer it is usually offset. [Why](/faq/ontario-new-home-affordability-payment).

**Land transfer tax rebates** — up to **$4,000** provincially, plus up to **$4,475** in Toronto.

Combined HST relief reaches roughly **$130,000** around a $1,000,000 purchase. [How it stacks](/guides/hst-rebate-guide-ontario-new-home).

## The test that disqualifies people

Read this carefully, because it costs buyers $50,000.

For the federal rebate, neither you **nor your spouse or common-law partner** can have **lived in a home either of you owned** — anywhere in the world — in the current calendar year or the four preceding years.

The disqualifier is **occupation of a home you or your partner owned**, not ownership alone.

- Never owned anything, but you live in your partner's condo → **disqualified**.
- Never owned anything, partner owns a rental neither of you lived in → **likely fine**.
- Owned a home, sold six years ago, renting since → **likely fine again**.

[The full trap](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer).

And note: the land transfer tax first-time buyer test is **not identical**. You can pass one and fail the other. Ask your lawyer about each separately.

## Why getting it wrong is worse than losing a rebate

Your price was almost certainly quoted [net of HST rebate](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning), with the rebate [assigned to the builder](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment). If CRA declines your claim, the builder bills you the amount **at closing** — up to $80,000 in certified funds, on the day, with no exit.

So "I think I qualify" is not good enough. Get it confirmed in writing by an accountant before you waive anything.

## The money you actually need

On a $900,000 freehold townhome:

- **Deposit:** typically 10% = **$90,000**, in instalments over 12–24 months, first draft on signing. [Structures](/faq/typical-deposit-structure-pre-construction-ontario)
- **Closing:** land transfer tax ~$12,475 (less up to $4,000 rebate), plus levies, connections, Tarion enrolment and legal fees → roughly **$25,000–$38,000**. [Breakdown](/guides/pre-construction-closing-cost-guide-ontario)
- **Not included in the price:** appliances, window coverings, fencing, rear landscaping → **$25,000–$60,000**. [What's excluded](/faq/what-is-included-in-a-new-low-rise-home)

Total beyond the mortgage: realistically **$140,000 to $190,000** spread over several years.

## The two risks that matter most to you

**No cooling-off period on freehold.** The agreement binds on acceptance. No ten days, no reconsidering. [Read this](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

**Financing is arranged years later.** Your pre-approval today does not bind any lender to fund a 2030 closing. If the home appraises below your price, you fund the gap in cash. First-time buyers with 10% down are the most exposed to this. [Why](/faq/mortgage-approval-closing-years-later).

## What to do, in order

1. **Confirm your first-time buyer status** with an accountant — federal rebate and land transfer tax separately.
2. **Get pre-qualified conservatively.** Buy below your maximum, not at it.
3. **Get represented before your first sales centre visit.** [It costs you nothing](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon).
4. **Identify a lawyer** who will review the agreement **before** you sign.
5. **Model the full cash requirement**, not just the deposit.
6. **Note the March 31, 2027 ENHR deadline** — it runs on the signing date.
