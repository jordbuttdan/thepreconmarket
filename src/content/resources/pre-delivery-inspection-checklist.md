The PDI is the most consequential two hours of the purchase and most buyers treat it as a tour. It is not a tour — it is the creation of a legal record.

Items **recorded** on the form are the builder's responsibility. Items not recorded are much harder to claim later, because the presumption is that they were not there when you took possession.

## Allow the time

If the builder has scheduled 45 minutes, say in advance that you need longer. Do not let their calendar set your thoroughness. You can also bring your own inspector, and on a home over $1,000,000 that is money well spent.

## The deadlines that follow

The PDI starts your warranty clock. The **30-day form** and the **year-end form** are your two main windows for first-year claims, each with its own hard deadline, and Tarion applies them strictly. Miss a window and the item generally stops being an enforceable claim. Diarise both on the day you close. [Coverage and deadlines](/faq/tarion-warranty-coverage-new-home).
