New construction closing costs run materially higher than resale, because a builder passes through a list of charges a private seller never bills you for.

## The working number

On a **$1,100,000 freehold home outside Toronto**, plan on **$30,000 to $45,000** at closing, on top of your deposit and mortgage. Inside the City of Toronto, add roughly $18,000 for the second land transfer tax.

Then add **$25,000 to $60,000** across the first year for the things the base price excludes — appliances, window coverings, fencing, rear landscaping, a deck. This is the most common budgeting error in new low-rise, because none of it appears on the price list. [What's excluded](/faq/what-is-included-in-a-new-low-rise-home).

## Ask for an estimate early

Your lawyer receives a **statement of adjustments** shortly before closing — often only a week or two, which is not enough time to raise money. Most lawyers will produce an estimate several months out if you ask. Doing so converts a nasty surprise into a plan. [What's on it](/faq/adjustments-on-closing-new-build).

## The item worth negotiating now

**Development charges.** The standard clause passes municipal increases through to you with no ceiling, and on a closing three or four years out that is genuinely open-ended. A cap makes it a number you can put in this worksheet. [Why it matters most](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap).
