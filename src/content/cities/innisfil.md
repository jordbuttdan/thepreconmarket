## What's being built in Innisfil

Innisfil is one of the most affordable new freehold markets within reach of the GTA, with most product between $570,000 and $980,000, almost entirely inside the full-rebate band. Alcona on Lake Simcoe carries most of the supply.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Alcona Shores (Bayview Wellington)
- Vicinity Homes Phase 2 (Mattamy)
- Lakehaven Towns (Mattamy)
- Churchill Downs Homes (First View)

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$566,000 to $978,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $566,000 | At $978,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$45,280** | **$78,240** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$28,300** | **$48,900** |

At the entry end, a home at $566,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $45,280, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Innisfil is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
