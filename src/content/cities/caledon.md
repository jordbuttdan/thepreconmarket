## What's being built in Caledon

Caledon is almost entirely a detached market, of the active communities here, nearly all are single family homes rather than towns. It is where buyers go for lot width and a greenbelt setting without leaving Peel, and the price band is broad enough that the rebate maths changes significantly between the entry and the top of a single community.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Windrose at Caledon Trails (Laurier/Yorkwood)
- Wildfield Village (Solmar)
- SouthCal by ARISTA
- SouthCal by OPUS
- Castles of Caledon (CountryWide)

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$699,000 to $2,250,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $699,000 | At $2,250,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$55,920** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$34,950** | **$0** |

At the entry end, a home at $699,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $55,920, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

At the top end, $2,250,000 is **at or above $1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate is **zero**. Watch this carefully: [lot premiums](/faq/lot-premiums-explained) and [upgrades](/faq/builder-upgrades-worth-it) count toward your price, so a base price just under the line can cross it at the décor appointment and cost more than the upgrades were worth. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

Above **$1,850,000** the enhanced Ontario rebate disappears entirely and you fall back to the long-standing [$24,000 standard rebate](/faq/hst-rebate-24000-standard). Estate product here crosses that line.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Caledon is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
