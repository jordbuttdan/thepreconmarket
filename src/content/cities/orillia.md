## What's being built in Orillia

Orillia has limited new low-rise supply and the product that exists is modest townhomes in the $490,000 to $590,000 range, comfortably inside the full rebate band. Between Lake Simcoe and Lake Couchiching, the market has a recreational component.

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$489,000 to $590,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $489,000 | At $590,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$39,120** | **$47,200** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$24,450** | **$29,500** |

At the entry end, a home at $489,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $39,120, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Orillia is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
