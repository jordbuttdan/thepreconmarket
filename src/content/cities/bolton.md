## What's being built in Bolton

Bolton is a small, almost entirely low-rise market inside Caledon, every active community here is detached or townhomes, with no condominium supply to speak of. Releases are infrequent and modest in size, which is why they tend to move fast when they come.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- North Hill by Solmar
- Livello in Bolton (Zancor)
- Meadowlark Enclave Homes (Treasure Hill)

Availability changes week to week and the best lots in any phase go early, [register for access](/vip) and I'll send what's releasing here.

**Builders active in Bolton:** Solmar Development Corp, Zancor Homes, Treasure Hill Homes, Dunsire Developments.

Builder choice matters more than most buyers expect. Delivery record, reliability on closing dates and how warranty items get handled vary enormously and appear on no price list. [How to assess a builder](/guides/builder-due-diligence-guide).

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$789,000 to $1,939,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $789,000 | At $1,939,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$63,120** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$39,450** | **$0** |

At the entry end, a home at $789,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $63,120, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

At the top end, $1,939,000 is **at or above $1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate is **zero**. Watch this carefully: [lot premiums](/faq/lot-premiums-explained) and [upgrades](/faq/builder-upgrades-worth-it) count toward your price, so a base price just under the line can cross it at the décor appointment and cost more than the upgrades were worth. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

Above **$1,850,000** the enhanced Ontario rebate disappears entirely and you fall back to the long-standing [$24,000 standard rebate](/faq/hst-rebate-24000-standard). Estate product here crosses that line.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Bolton is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
