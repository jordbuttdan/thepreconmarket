## What's being built in Bradford West Gwillimbury

Bradford is the first Simcoe market north of the York Region line and prices reflect that, higher than Barrie, lower than East Gwillimbury. Supply is moderate and skews detached, with Bond Head a distinct and quieter sub-market to the west.

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$864,000 to $1,483,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $864,000 | At $1,483,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$69,120** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$43,200** | **$1,700** |

At the entry end, a home at $864,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $69,120, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Bradford West Gwillimbury is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
