## What's being built in Essa

Essa — principally Angus and Thornton — has no significant new low-rise supply currently tracked. It is a small township west of Barrie, anchored by CFB Borden, where new construction tends to be individual builds rather than subdivision releases.

## What you could claim here

Even with no active communities, the programs are worth understanding before you widen your search, because they are time-limited and they run on your **signing date**.

The [Ontario Enhanced New Housing Rebate](/faq/ontario-enhanced-new-housing-rebate) pays the 8% provincial portion of HST up to **$80,000** on a new home, and it has **no first-time buyer test**. The [federal first-time buyer GST rebate](/faq/first-time-home-buyers-gst-rebate) adds up to **$50,000** on top for those who qualify. Both require a new build, and both require the home to be your primary residence.

The Ontario window closes **March 31, 2027**, and it is the signing date that counts, not the closing date. [The deadline](/faq/hst-rebate-deadline-march-2027).


## Land transfer tax

Essa is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay **Ontario land transfer tax only** — no second municipal tax. On a $1,100,000 home that is about **$18,475**, against roughly $37,000 for the same home inside Toronto. First-time buyers can claim up to **$4,000** back.

It is payable in cash on closing and cannot be financed, so it belongs in your budget from the start alongside [levies and adjustments](/guides/pre-construction-closing-cost-guide-ontario). [Full rates](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).

## Getting around

Highway 90 to Barrie and Highway 400, with no GO rail service.

Worth being honest with yourself about this. New low-rise communities are car-dependent at launch almost without exception — the retail, transit and services that make an area convenient arrive years after the homes do, and sometimes differently than the plan showed.

## Where to look instead

If Essa is where you want to be, it is worth watching — sites do come forward. In the meantime, these nearby markets have active new freehold low-rise:

- [Barrie](/new-homes/barrie) — strong supply, roughly $694,000 to $1,594,000
