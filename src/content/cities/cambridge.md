## What's being built in Cambridge

Cambridge is the most affordable of the three Waterloo Region cities for new freehold, with most product between $530,000 and $860,000. Built from three historic towns, Galt, Preston and Hespeler, it still reads as three distinct places.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Westwood Village (Cachet)
- Marquis Series at Hazel Glenn (Treasure Hill)
- Modal At Main Towns (Reids Heritage)

**Builders active in Cambridge:** Reids Heritage Homes, Treasure Hill Homes, Cachet Homes, JD Development Group, Laurelcrest Homes.

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$534,000 to $860,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $534,000 | At $860,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$42,720** | **$68,800** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$26,700** | **$43,000** |

At the entry end, a home at $534,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $42,720, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Cambridge is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
