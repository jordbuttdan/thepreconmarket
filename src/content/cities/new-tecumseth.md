## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$779,000 to $1,300,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $779,000 | At $1,300,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$62,320** | **$80,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$38,950** | **$20,000** |

At the entry end, a home at $779,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $62,320, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

New Tecumseth is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
