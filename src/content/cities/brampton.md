## What's being built in Brampton

Brampton is one of the two or three highest-volume freehold low-rise markets in the GTA, and the range is unusually wide — urban towns under $500,000 at one end, Castlemore estate detached past $2,000,000 at the other. Mayfield Village and the Countryside corridor carry most of the current activity.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Navera at Mayfield Village (DiGreen)
- Royal Castlemore (Umbria)
- Countryside Urban Towns (Rosehaven)
- Cornerstone (Primont)
- Spruce Trails Towns (Camcos)
- Rosemont Grove (Hallett)

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$397,000 to $1,810,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $397,000 | At $1,810,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$31,760** | **$30,400** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$19,850** | **$0** |

At the entry end, a home at $397,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate — $31,760 — plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

At the top end, $1,810,000 is **at or above $1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate is **zero**. Watch this carefully: [lot premiums](/faq/lot-premiums-explained) and [upgrades](/faq/builder-upgrades-worth-it) count toward your price, so a base price just under the line can cross it at the décor appointment and cost more than the upgrades were worth. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you — it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).


These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Brampton is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay **Ontario land transfer tax only** — no second municipal tax. On a $1,100,000 home that is about **$18,475**, against roughly $37,000 for the same home inside Toronto. First-time buyers can claim up to **$4,000** back.

It is payable in cash on closing and cannot be financed, so it belongs in your budget from the start alongside [levies and adjustments](/guides/pre-construction-closing-cost-guide-ontario). [Full rates](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).

## Before you sign anything in Brampton

Freehold purchases in Ontario have **no cooling-off period**. The ten-day rescission right buyers have heard about is a condominium provision and does not apply to a detached home, semi or freehold townhome. Once the builder accepts your agreement, you are bound. [This is the one to understand](/faq/cooling-off-period-freehold-ontario).

So the protection has to be in place beforehand:

- **Have a lawyer read the agreement before you sign**, not after. [What they look for](/faq/lawyer-review-agreement-pre-construction)
- **Ask for a cap on development charges and levies** — the most valuable amendment available on a closing years out. [Why](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap)
- **Confirm your rebate eligibility** rather than assuming it. If your price is quoted net of the rebate and you do not qualify, the builder bills you at closing. [How that works](/faq/builder-keeps-hst-rebate-assignment)
- **Check your deposit against Tarion's limits** — freehold protection caps at 10% of price up to $100,000. [Deposit protection](/faq/is-my-deposit-protected-freehold-ontario)
- **Register for representation before your first sales centre visit.** The builder budgets the commission either way. [Why it costs you nothing](/faq/does-it-cost-more-to-use-a-realtor-precon)
