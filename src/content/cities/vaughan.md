## What's being built in Vaughan

Vaughan is the strongest new freehold low-rise market in the GTA, and the deepest. Kleinburg and Woodbridge carry the premium detached product, Vellore Village and Patterson the mid-market towns and singles. The spread between an entry townhome and a Kleinburg estate home is wide enough that two buyers in the same city land in completely different rebate bands.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Bridgelands (Arista/DECO/OPUS)
- Weston Woods (Paradise)
- UpTown Woodbridge (Fieldgate)
- Collection on 27 (UNIQ)
- Valley Brooke Towns (Tiffany Park)
- Balmoral Estates (Lormel)
- Vellore Trails (Arista)

**Builders active in Vaughan:** Arista Homes, DECO Homes, OPUS Homes, Paradise Developments, Fieldgate Homes, UNIQ Communities, Tiffany Park Homes, CountryWide Homes, Royal Pine Homes, Lormel Homes.

Builder choice matters more than most buyers expect. Delivery record, reliability on closing dates and how warranty items get handled vary enormously and appear on no price list. [How to assess a builder](/guides/builder-due-diligence-guide).

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$699,000 to $2,439,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $699,000 | At $2,439,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$55,920** | **$0** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$34,950** | **$0** |

At the entry end, a home at $699,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $55,920, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

The band here straddles **$1,000,000**, which is where the structure changes: above it the Ontario rebate stops growing and sits flat at **$80,000**, while the federal rebate begins tapering toward zero at $1,500,000. Two buyers in the same community can be in different places.

At the top end, $2,439,000 is **at or above $1,500,000**, where the federal first-time buyer rebate is **zero**. Watch this carefully: [lot premiums](/faq/lot-premiums-explained) and [upgrades](/faq/builder-upgrades-worth-it) count toward your price, so a base price just under the line can cross it at the décor appointment and cost more than the upgrades were worth. [The price cliffs](/faq/hst-rebate-over-1-5-million).

Above **$1,850,000** the enhanced Ontario rebate disappears entirely and you fall back to the long-standing [$24,000 standard rebate](/faq/hst-rebate-24000-standard). Estate product here crosses that line.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Vaughan is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).

Highways 400, 407 and 427, the Barrie GO line, and the TTC subway at the Vaughan Metropolitan Centre.
