## What's being built in Guelph

Guelph's current new low-rise supply is overwhelmingly townhomes rather than detached, priced between roughly $480,000 and $660,000. That is squarely in the band where the full 8% Ontario rebate applies and, for first-time buyers, the full federal rebate as well.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- Clairmont Towns (Mattamy)
- Thrive Towns (Marann)
- Northside Townhouse (Granite)

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$484,000 to $660,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $484,000 | At $660,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$38,720** | **$52,800** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$24,200** | **$33,000** |

At the entry end, a home at $484,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $38,720, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Guelph is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
