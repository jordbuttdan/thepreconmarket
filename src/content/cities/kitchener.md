## What's being built in Kitchener

Kitchener has deep new low-rise supply, concentrated in the southwest around Doon and Huron Park. Pricing runs from about $400,000 for towns to just over $900,000 for detached, almost entirely within the full Ontario rebate band.

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$400,000 to $920,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $400,000 | At $920,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$32,000** | **$73,600** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$20,000** | **$46,000** |

At the entry end, a home at $400,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $32,000, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

Kitchener is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
