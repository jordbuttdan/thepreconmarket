## What's being built in St. Catharines

St. Catharines has the lowest entry pricing of any market on this site, AXIS Towns start around $330,000. At that price the full 8% Ontario rebate and, for a first-time buyer, the full federal rebate both apply, which is a materially different proposition from the GTA.

Communities that have launched or are actively selling here include:

- AXIS Towns Phase 1 (Phelps)
- Lot 16 Towns (Sphere)
- Bromley Gardens (Dunsire)

**Builders active in St. Catharines:** Phelps Homes, Dunsire Developments, Sphere Developments, Marydel Homes.

Builder choice matters more than most buyers expect. Delivery record, reliability on closing dates and how warranty items get handled vary enormously and appear on no price list. [How to assess a builder](/guides/builder-due-diligence-guide).

## What the rebates are worth at local prices

New low-rise here currently runs roughly **$329,000 to $825,000**, and that band decides your rebate position. Here is the arithmetic at both ends.

| | At $329,000 | At $825,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario ENHR (no first-time buyer test) | **$26,320** | **$66,000** |
| Federal rebate (first-time buyers only) | **$16,450** | **$41,250** |

At the entry end, a home at $329,000 sits **below $1,000,000**, which means the **full 8%** Ontario rebate, $26,320, plus the full 5% federal rebate for a first-time buyer. This is the most favourable band in the whole structure.

If you have **owned a home before**, note that the Ontario rebate still applies to you, it has no first-time buyer test, and move-up buyers routinely assume otherwise and claim nothing. [Read this one](/faq/do-i-have-to-be-first-time-buyer-ontario-rebate).

These are illustrative maximums at the ends of the local range, not a quote. [Run your own numbers](/hst-rebate/checker).

## Land transfer tax

St. Catharines is outside the City of Toronto, so you pay Ontario land transfer tax only, with no second municipal tax. [Rates and the first-time buyer refund](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).
