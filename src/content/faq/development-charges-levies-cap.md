Municipalities charge builders for the infrastructure new housing requires — water, sewers, roads, transit, parks, schools. Those are **development charges**, and in almost every Ontario builder agreement they are passed through to you.

## Why the clause is dangerous on low-rise

A typical clause says you will pay any **increase** in development charges, levies and imposts between the date of the agreement and closing.

On a resale purchase that is meaningless. On a low-rise pre-construction purchase closing three or four years out, it is an open-ended obligation whose size is decided by a municipal council you have no relationship with, after you are already bound.

Charges in GTA municipalities have moved substantially over the past decade. An uncapped clause has produced five-figure closing surprises, and the buyer has no recourse — they agreed to it.

## The amendment to ask for

A **capped levy amendment** states a maximum: "development charges, levies and imposts capped at $12,000." Anything above that is the builder's problem.

This converts an unknown liability into a budgetable number, and it is the **single most valuable thing to negotiate** in a builder agreement. More valuable than a décor allowance, more valuable than an appliance package, and considerably more valuable than the small price concessions buyers usually chase.

## What is achievable

It depends on the project's sales position, not on persistence:

- **Hot early release.** Often nothing. The builder has other buyers.
- **Mid-phase.** A cap is frequently available, sometimes just by asking.
- **Slow inventory.** A cap plus other concessions is realistic.

Where a full cap is refused, ask for a **partial** cap, or for a cap on the largest component. Something beats nothing.

## Check what the cap covers

Caps vary in scope. Read it carefully — ideally your lawyer does:

- Does it cover **all** levies and imposts, or only development charges?
- Are **utility connections, meters and Tarion enrolment** inside or outside it?
- Does it cover **education levies** and parkland dedication?
- Is it per home, or does it apply to a bundle?

A cap that excludes the three largest items is decoration.

## If you already signed without one

Ask your lawyer to confirm the exact exposure and, several months before closing, to obtain an **estimate** of the levy component from the builder. You cannot change the clause, but you can stop being surprised by it — and you can plan the cash.
