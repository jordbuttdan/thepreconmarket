The pre-delivery inspection is the most consequential two hours of the entire purchase, and most buyers treat it as a tour.

It is not a tour. It is the creation of a legal record.

## What it is

Shortly before closing you walk the finished home with a builder representative and complete the **PDI form**. That form records the condition of the home at handover and becomes the **baseline** against which warranty claims are assessed.

Damage or missing items **recorded** on the form are the builder's responsibility. Damage not recorded is much harder to claim later, because the presumption is that it was not there when you took possession.

## Prepare properly

**Bring someone.** Two sets of eyes, and ideally one that has done this before. You can bring your own inspector; many buyers do, and on a $1M+ home it is money well spent.

**Bring your documents.** The agreement schedules, the specifications, your upgrade and selection orders. The point is to check the house against **what you bought**, not against what looks acceptable. [What was included](/faq/what-is-included-in-a-new-low-rise-home).

**Allow two to three hours.** If the builder has budgeted 45 minutes, say in advance that you need longer. Do not let the schedule set your thoroughness.

**Bring a tape measure, a phone charger to test outlets, and a flashlight.**

**Photograph everything.** Date-stamped photos of every room, every surface, the basement, the mechanicals and the exterior.

## What to check

- **Every outlet, switch, and light fixture.**
- **Every tap, drain and toilet**, run them, check pressure and for leaks under sinks.
- **Furnace, HVAC, hot water tank, HRV**, on, running, and the models matching your paperwork.
- **Windows and doors**, open, close, lock, latch. Check screens.
- **Floors**, scratches, lifting, hollow spots, squeaks.
- **Walls and ceilings**, drywall seams, nail pops, paint coverage.
- **Cabinets and counters**, doors aligned, chips, scratches, the specified finishes.
- **Appliances**, present, correct models, functioning.
- **Basement**, moisture, cracks, floor drain, sump.
- **Exterior**, brick and siding, caulking, soffits, downspouts, garage door operation.
- **Grading, sodding and driveway**, note what is **not** done. Much of this is legitimately deferred, but record the state. [Timelines](/faq/grading-sodding-driveway-timeline).
- **Upgrades**, every single one you paid for, individually confirmed.

## Rules for the form

**Write down everything, however small.** A scratch you overlook is a scratch you own.

**Do not accept "we'll take care of it" verbally.** If it is not on the form, it does not exist.

**Do not let anyone tell you an item is "normal" and leave it off.** Record it; the determination comes later.

**Keep your copy**, signed, with your photos.

## Afterwards

The PDI starts your warranty clock. The **30-day form** and the **year-end form** are your main first-year claim windows, each with its own deadline. [Coverage and deadlines](/faq/tarion-warranty-coverage-new-home).

Diarise both the day you close.
