There are two separate piles of money, separated by several years, and people routinely budget only the first.

## Pile one: the deposit

Commonly **10% of the price on freehold**, sometimes 15% or 20%, paid in instalments over 12 to 24 months. On a $1,100,000 home at 10%, that is **$110,000**, with the first $25,000 to $50,000 due as a bank draft on signing. [Typical schedules](/faq/typical-deposit-structure-pre-construction-ontario).

## Pile two: closing

Due years later, in certified funds, on top of your mortgage:

| Item | On a $1.1M freehold home |
| --- | --- |
| Ontario land transfer tax | ~$18,475 |
| Toronto municipal LTT (city only) | ~$18,475 more |
| Development charges / levies | $5,000 – $20,000+, uncapped unless negotiated |
| Utility connections, meters, Tarion enrolment | $2,000 – $5,000 |
| Legal fees and disbursements | $2,500 – $4,000 |
| Grading / sodding deposits | $1,000 – $3,000 |

Outside Toronto, a realistic all-in closing figure on this home is roughly **$30,000 to $45,000**. In Toronto, add the municipal land transfer tax. First-time buyers can claim up to **$4,000** provincially and up to **$4,475** in Toronto. [More](/faq/land-transfer-tax-new-home-ontario).

## The two items that can blow the budget

**An uncapped levy clause.** Development charges rising between signing and a closing four years out, passed through with no ceiling. Get a cap. [More](/faq/development-charges-levies-cap).

**A failed HST rebate.** If your price was quoted [net of HST rebate](/faq/price-net-of-hst-rebate-meaning) and you turn out not to qualify, because you are [renting it out](/faq/hst-rebate-rental-investment-property), or the [spousal test](/faq/spouse-owned-home-first-time-buyer) catches you, the builder bills the rebate amount at closing. On this home that is up to **$80,000**, due on the day.

## And the one nobody budgets

**An appraisal shortfall.** If the home appraises below your price, the gap is cash. [Why this happens](/faq/mortgage-approval-closing-years-later).

## A working rule

For a $1.1M freehold home outside Toronto, plan on roughly **$110,000 in deposits** and **$35,000 to $45,000 at closing**, around $150,000 total, excluding any rebate or appraisal surprise. Hold a reserve beyond that.

If that number is uncomfortable, the answer is a less expensive home, not a tighter budget. The people who get hurt here are the ones with no margin. [Let's build the actual number for your purchase](/book).
